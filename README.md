# My Favorite Series :heartpulse:
## High Potential
---
**Genre:** Crime :mag:, Comedy-Drama :joy: :cry:
**Main Character:** Morgan Gillory
---

## Movie Decription :pencil2:
*Morgan* is a cleaner with very high IQ. She was hired by the police because they saw a potential in her that she can solve crimes that nobody else can figure out. She solves crimes using her mind and logic. The movie is actually based on French series **HPI**.

## :thought_balloon: My favorite part of the movie 
It is when Morgan encountered a killer that uses the same logic as hers. It took a while before she figured out who the killer is. She encountered several puzzles wherein it requires high IQ in order to understand how things work and what are the next step to know where the victim is. She really showed her intelligence in solving crimes and in the end they found out who is behind all the crimes.
